Execution Workflow

The End-to-End Examination Lifecycle

From institutional structure setup and routine scheduling to script evaluation, teacher verification, and official tabulation.

1

Institutional Governance & AI Routine Ingestion

Chief Exam Controller sets up Colleges, Departments, and Course rosters, then scans university exam routines via AI to automatically schedule examination sessions.

2

23-Taxonomy OBE Question Paper & Master Solution Studio

Course instructor authors question papers with Bloom's taxonomy, CO/PO classifications, rubric criteria, and uploads golden master benchmark solutions.

3

Dual Evaluation Pipelines: AI Wizard or Direct Manual

Faculty uploads student scripts. Choose AI Evaluation Wizard (300 DPI OCR, auto-boundary mapping, multi-provider AI scoring) or Fast-Track Manual Grading Wizard (instant page slicing without AI).

4

Split-Screen Teacher Verification & PDF Certification

Faculty inspects scanned script side-by-side with rubric criteria, overrides marks if needed, and finalizes the evaluation to stamp certified watermarked PDFs.

5

Live OBE Course Tabulation, 8-Sheet Excel & Student Transparency

Live sync aggregates multi-assessment scores into course grade records, compiles official 8-sheet Excel files, and opens transparency dashboards for students.
