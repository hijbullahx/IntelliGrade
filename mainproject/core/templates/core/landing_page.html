The End-to-End Evaluation Workflow

From exam setup by teachers to final result inspection by students and recheck management.

1

Exam Creation & Rubric Definition

Teacher creates an examination, attaches question prompts, assigns marks, Bloom's levels, CO/PO mappings, and rubric criteria.

2

Answer Script Upload & OCR Segmentation

Scanned student answer scripts are uploaded. OpenCV contour line cropping and CRNN/SimpleHTR extract handwritten text per question segment.

3

AI Rubric Evaluation & Confidence Score

Extracted answers are compared against model solutions using Gemini AI. The engine suggests marks, confidence ratings, and feedback.

4

Teacher Review & Grade Publication

Teacher verifies every AI suggestion in the Evaluation Workbench, modifies marks if needed, and publishes finalized results.

Student Inspection & Recheck Requests

Students log into their portal to view evaluated scripts and feedback. They can submit recheck requests which teachers review directly.

The Modern Approach to Academic Evaluation

See how IntelliGrade transforms traditional manual paper grading into an outcome-based digital workflow.

Traditional Paper Grading

  • Time-Consuming: Physical sorting, manual reading, and paper mark tallying.
  • No OBE Insights: Manual calculation of Bloom's levels and CO/PO attainment is tedious.
  • Limited Feedback: Minimal written feedback provided to students on script papers.
  • Paper Recheck Hassle: Students must physically request script reviews with manual re-evaluations.

With IntelliGrade

  • Efficient OCR & AI: Auto-segmentation and HTR pipeline speed up grading.
  • OBE & Bloom's Analytics: Automated CO/PO attainment reports for Department Heads.
  • Rich Feedback & Criteria: Students view question-level marks and detailed AI comments.
  • Digital Recheck Workflow: Students submit online recheck requests; teachers re-evaluate in workbench.
