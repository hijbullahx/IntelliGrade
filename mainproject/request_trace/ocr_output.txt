3. A university office is preparing to scan and store three documents: a black-and-white attendance sheet, a
colored event photo, and a student's ID card scan. The university authority has decided to prepare a report
for technical specifications and future reusability, the report should i) explain the different types of digital
images that may correspond to the files described above, ii) explain the technique used for representing
digital images in computers, and iii) state the commonly used digital image formats suitable for storing and
processing each of the above files. Write and prepare the report for the university. [CO1, C2, PO1 — 8 + 10
+ 7 = 25 marks]

enhancing image analysis, pattern recognition, and classification tasks associated with these two steps.
